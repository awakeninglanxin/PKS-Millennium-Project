## 数学推导

### Young-Laplace 与平均曲率

理想液滴: 表面张力平衡 → 平均曲率 H 处处相等:

$$\Delta p = \sigma \left(\frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}\right) = 2\sigma H$$

对于旋转体母线 $r(z)$:

$$H(z) = \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{r''(z)}{(1+r'(z)^2)^{3/2}} + \frac{1}{r(z)\sqrt{1+r'(z)^2}}\right]$$

$$c_{\text{inv}}(\theta) = \frac{1}{\frac{e^{i\theta}}{2} - \frac{e^{2i\theta}}{4}}$$
